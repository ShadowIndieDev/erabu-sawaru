## 使用プロンプト

Use case: scientific-educational. Asset type: one illustration atlas for a Japanese school augmentative communication app, to be displayed as 8 cropped choice cards using CSS. Produce a perfectly regular 4 column by 2 row grid, landscape 2:1 overall aspect ratio. All eight cells must be equal squares, no borders, no gutters, no text, no labels, no watermark. Entire background is the same clean pure white. In each cell, center one easily recognizable subject with generous 18 percent white padding so it is never cut off. Top row, left to right: one red apple with a small green leaf; one bunch of two ripe yellow bananas; one triangular Japanese white rice onigiri with a black nori seaweed rectangle; one small plain golden bread roll. Bottom row, left to right: one friendly realistic brown dog sitting, full body; one gray tabby cat sitting, full body; one white rabbit sitting with both long ears upright, full body; one gray elephant standing in side three-quarter view, full body including trunk and four legs. Style: clear, polished naturalistic watercolor and colored-pencil educational illustration with restrained soft shadows; mature neutral picture-card style suitable for secondary-school students, not babyish, not anthropomorphic, no clothing or decorations. Keep backgrounds empty and visual scale consistent. Exact cell order is essential. This is a single sprite atlas, not an app screenshot.
